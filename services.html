Aircraft class guide

Which class is yours?

Prices are set by aircraft class. If yours sits between two, pick the closest or choose Not sure on the request form and we'll sort it out.

  • Single engine

    One piston engine. Trainers, touring and personal aircraft.

    Typical examples: Cessna 172, Cessna 182, Piper Cherokee, Cirrus SR22

  • Twin / turboprop

    Piston twins, plus single and twin turboprops.

    Typical examples: Beechcraft Baron, Piper Seneca, King Air, Pilatus PC-12

  • Light jet

    Smaller business jets, often flown single pilot.

    Typical examples: Citation CJ series, Embraer Phenom 300, HondaJet

  • Mid / large jet

    Midsize, super midsize and large-cabin business jets.

    Typical examples: Citation Latitude, Challenger 350, Gulfstream G450

Not sure what you need?

Tell us about your aircraft, where it's based and what it needs, and we'll work out the right service with you. A quick call usually settles it.
